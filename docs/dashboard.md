### Basement Dashboard (Lovelace, `basement.yaml`)
- **Structure**: Sections-view dashboard (`path: basement`) assembled from `dashboards/components/*.yaml` via `!include`, built on `custom:mushroom-template-card` / `custom:bubble-card` primitives with heavy `card_mod` styling. Repeated card shapes live in `components/decluttering_templates.yaml`: `avr_volume_slider` (header and remote), `theater_mode_button`, `quick_mood_pill`, `effect_pill`, `announcer_preset_button`, `spotify_preset_button`, and the remote's `remote_dpad_button`, `remote_transport_button`, `remote_app_button` and `spacer_card`. Decluttering templates only wrap whole cards; chips inside a `mushroom-chips-card` (header buttons, the remote's mode and DSP rows) can't use them.
- **Layout (2026-11-11)**: lighting is the first column (top on a phone) and the theater column second, because modes normally start from the Onn remote (docs/onn-remote.md). The theater card is the now-playing marquee, one compact row of four mode pills (`theater_mode_button`, phone fallback and active-mode indicator), then the receiver, Curtain Call and the game/music extras while in use.
- **Header cockpit**: 3-zone chip row (left: home nav; center: 1-tap lighting-mode toggle, hold to reset; right: conditional utility chips — play/pause (state from `binary_sensor.theater_media_playing`, hidden in game mode), remote pop-up launcher, basement blackout), then the AVR master volume slider while the theater is in use, and the booth announcer bar, shown while `binary_sensor.theater_announcer_bar` is on (sports mode, sports apps, or Prime Video for TNF).
- **"Now Playing" hero card** (Section 2): conditional on `binary_sensor.theater_in_use == on` and `sensor.theater_mode` in `[game, movie, sports, music]`.
  - **Large Rectangular Poster Format**: Upgraded to a 320px theatrical hero marquee. Features an uncropped 275px x 185px prominent vertical theatrical poster standing on the left with 3D drop-shadow and mode ambient glow, beside a full widescreen blurred backdrop with bold title and metadata typography. Movie search has been removed to keep the interface and remote modal clean and clutter-free.
- **Zone Lighting & Quick Moods**:
  - **Palettes and effects (2026-11-11)**: a 2x2 grid of palette pills (`quick_mood_pill`: Speakeasy, Synthwave, Cyberpunk, Nordic; the active one glows while in Social Mode), then a row of three `effect_pill`s: Shifting (22s drift toggle), Toast flash on the bar and stairs, Eagles chant in Kelly Green (highlighted during a live game or sports Social Mode). The card's header line shows the mode and palette; the Social/Daily Driver toggle is the header chip. Below, three zone sliders: Lounge & Bar (9-bulb group, with bar and lounge sub-buttons), Theater (ceiling dimmer and screen bias sub-buttons), Stairlights (candle 2% and full presets). Each slider is tinted with its light's own color when on (amber if it reports none) and neutral when off.
- **Retro Game Box Art (Libretro Open Scraper)**: In addition to SteamGridDB and RAWG, `_fetch_game_art_from_secrets` now directly queries the official `libretro-thumbnails` repository (`Nintendo_-_Nintendo_64`, `Sony_-_PlayStation`, `Sega_-_Dreamcast`, etc.) with zero API key dependencies. Games like *NFL Blitz 2000* resolve authentic high-res box art and in-game snapshots rather than generic system clip art.
- **Spotify Art Stale Cache Fix**: In `update_theater_media_art()`, sports mode no longer falls back to `onn_art` (which held stale Spotify album art), and movie mode actively purges lingering Spotify media sessions. Mode initiations immediately clear previous session artwork with mode-specific theatrical art.
- **App Launching**: `launch_onn_app()` launches through the Android TV Remote (deep link such as `stremio://` first, then the package name; HDHomeRun gets no deep link because the Onn rejects `hdhomerun://` and the error drops the remote connection, losing the package launch), falling back to an ADB `monkey` launch on `media_player.theater_onn_pro_adb` only when `remote.onn_pro` is unavailable. Launches are preceded by a 1.0s wake delay to ensure the Android TV Remote connection is established before launching. (The unsupported `media_player.select_source` fallback on the Onn was removed.)
- **Music Mode Conduction**: `play_music()` engages Yamaha **7ch Stereo**. With the projector off it keeps it off and leaves the Onn alone (phone streams to the AVR); with the projector on it opens Spotify on the Onn.
- **This dashboard is tracked here rather than in a separate file** since it's the most significant/actively-iterated dashboard; if other dashboards (bedroom, kitchen/good-morning, diagnostics) grow to a comparable level of detail and churn, consider splitting each into its own file at that point rather than growing this one further.
