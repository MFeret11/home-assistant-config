### Basement Dashboard (Lovelace, `basement.yaml`)
- **Structure**: Sections-view dashboard (`path: basement`) assembled from `dashboards/components/*.yaml` via `!include`, built on `custom:mushroom-template-card` / `custom:bubble-card` primitives with heavy `card_mod` styling. Repeated card shapes live in `components/decluttering_templates.yaml`: `avr_volume_slider` (header and remote), `theater_mode_button`, `effect_pill`, the Themes pop-up's `theme_slot_pill`, `theme_tab_button` and `theme_bulb_button`, `announcer_preset_button`, `spotify_preset_button`, and the remote's `remote_dpad_button`, `remote_transport_button`, `remote_app_button` and `spacer_card`. Decluttering templates only wrap whole cards; chips inside a `mushroom-chips-card` (header buttons, the remote's mode and DSP rows) can't use them.
- **Layout (2026-11-11)**: lighting is the first column (top on a phone) and the theater column second, because modes normally start from the Onn remote (docs/onn-remote.md). The theater card is the now-playing marquee, one compact row of four mode pills (`theater_mode_button`, phone fallback and active-mode indicator), then the receiver, Curtain Call and the game/music extras while in use. While the theater is idle, an Up Next card (`components/up_next_trakt.yaml`) lists the next unwatched episode of each show from `sensor.trakt_next_to_watch_all`.
- **Header cockpit**: 3-zone chip row (left: home nav; center: 1-tap lighting-mode toggle, hold to reset; right: conditional utility chips — play/pause (state from `binary_sensor.theater_media_playing`, hidden in game mode), remote pop-up launcher, basement blackout), then the AVR master volume slider while the theater is in use, and the booth announcer bar, shown while `binary_sensor.theater_announcer_bar` is on (sports mode, sports apps, or Prime Video for TNF).
- **"Now Playing" hero card** (Section 2): conditional on `binary_sensor.theater_in_use == on` and `sensor.theater_mode` in `[game, movie, sports, music]`. The same card is included twice (2026-11-27): phones (under 768px wide, where the columns stack lighting-first) show it in the header section right under the volume bar, so the poster doesn't sit below the lighting card; wider screens show it at the top of the theater column.
  - **Large Rectangular Poster Format**: Upgraded to a 320px theatrical hero marquee. Features an uncropped 275px x 185px prominent vertical theatrical poster standing on the left with 3D drop-shadow and mode ambient glow, beside a full widescreen blurred backdrop with bold title and metadata typography. Movie search has been removed to keep the interface and remote modal clean and clutter-free.
- **Zone Lighting & Quick Moods**:
  - **Themes and effects (2026-11-27)**: a Themes chip on the right of the card's header row (the header's second line names the theme in use) opens the `#themes` pop-up, `components/theme_library_popup.yaml`. The pop-up never lists themes itself: basement.py fills 12 fixed pills (`pyscript.basement_theme_slot_N`) with whatever matches the search box (`input_text.basement_theme_search`) and filter (All, Moods, Holidays, Mine), a page at a time, with each theme's name, icon and swatch. So a new built-in needs only its table entry in `pyscript/modules/basement_palettes.py`. Tap a pill to paint it; hold it to edit. The editor (shown while `pyscript.basement_theme_editor` isn't `off`) paints the theme and lists All room bulbs, All stairs and each of the 12 bulbs (tap for its color picker), then a name box and Save, Save as new, Delete (ours) or Reset to original (an edited built-in, marked ✎), and Cancel. "New from current lights" opens it on the lights as they are. The pop-up opens with a Moments row of two `effect_pill`s (moved off the lighting card 2026-11-27): Toast flash on the bar and stairs, Eagles chant in Kelly Green (highlighted during a live game or sports Social Mode; also Guide-hold on the Onn remote). The card's header line shows the mode and palette; the Social/Daily Driver toggle is the header chip. Below, three zone sliders: Lounge & Bar (9-bulb group, with bar and lounge sub-buttons), Theater (ceiling dimmer and screen bias sub-buttons), Stairlights (candle 2% and full presets). Each slider is tinted with its light's own color when on (amber if it reports none) and neutral when off.
- **Retro Game Box Art (Libretro Open Scraper)**: In addition to SteamGridDB and RAWG, `_fetch_game_art_from_secrets` now directly queries the official `libretro-thumbnails` repository (`Nintendo_-_Nintendo_64`, `Sony_-_PlayStation`, `Sega_-_Dreamcast`, etc.) with zero API key dependencies. Games like *NFL Blitz 2000* resolve authentic high-res box art and in-game snapshots rather than generic system clip art.
- **Spotify Art Stale Cache Fix**: In `update_theater_media_art()`, sports mode no longer falls back to `onn_art` (which held stale Spotify album art), and movie mode actively purges lingering Spotify media sessions. Mode initiations immediately clear previous session artwork with mode-specific theatrical art.
- **App Launching**: `launch_onn_app()` launches through the Android TV Remote (deep link such as `stremio://` first, then the package name; HDHomeRun gets no deep link because the Onn rejects `hdhomerun://` and the error drops the remote connection, losing the package launch), falling back to an ADB `monkey` launch on `media_player.theater_onn_pro_adb` only when `remote.onn_pro` is unavailable. Launches are preceded by a 1.0s wake delay to ensure the Android TV Remote connection is established before launching. (The unsupported `media_player.select_source` fallback on the Onn was removed.)
- **Music mode**: `play_music()` engages Yamaha **7ch Stereo**. With the projector off it keeps it off and leaves the Onn alone (phone streams to the AVR); with the projector on it opens Spotify on the Onn.
- **This dashboard is tracked here rather than in a separate file** since it's the most significant/actively-iterated dashboard; if other dashboards (bedroom, kitchen/good-morning, diagnostics) grow to a comparable level of detail and churn, consider splitting each into its own file at that point rather than growing this one further.
